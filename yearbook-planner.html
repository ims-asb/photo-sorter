Yearbook Layout Planner

Back to school: a big photo, two tall photos, and a real filmstrip. Pick a straight strip, or a strip that bends around the pages in an O. Runs only on this computer. No photos needed.

The star photo is the big one top left. It is about 2.5 times the area of each tall photo, which is the usual rule.
The fold. Nothing sits in the middle strip. A filmstrip is the only thing that crosses the fold, and it has no photo there, so no face gets cut.
The strip picture. "Download the strip picture" saves the black film (holes cut out, see-through) as a PNG the size of the spread. Put it on the page as a graphic, then place your photos on top in the frames.
Filmstrip order. Read left to right across both pages, like a story: arriving, hallway, lunch, class, after school.
Photo count. The count under the picture follows your choices. Empty film with no photo still looks right, so you can leave a stretch blank.

Red dashed line: trim (where the page is cut). Blue dashed line: safe margin. Keep faces and text inside the blue line. Let any photo that touches the edge run to the red line plus the bleed your printer asks for.
